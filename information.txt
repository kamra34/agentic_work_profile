AI Resume Tailoring Platform
Product Overview
• Intelligent CV Customization Automatically analyzes job descriptions using dual AI models
 (OpenAI GPT-4o + Anthropic Claude) to extract requirements, then tailors your master CV profile
  by intelligently selecting and highlighting the most relevant experiences, skills, and achievements for each specific job application. 
• Real-time ATS Optimization Provides instant ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility scores and fit analysis from both AI
   models, with live recalculation as you customize content visibility. Interactive preview lets you hide/show specific bullet points
   , roles, or entire sections while monitoring how changes impact your scores before generating the final PDF. 
• Professional PDF Generation Converts your tailored content into clean, ATS-friendly PDF resumes with proper formatting,
    hierarchical structure (company → role → achievements), and customizable visibility controls. 
    Download production-ready CVs optimized for both automated screening systems and human recruiters.
• Application Tracking Dashboard Manage your entire job search pipeline with status tracking (Draft → Applied → Interview → Offer),
 interview scheduling, company details, salary ranges, and historical ATS scores. Visual dashboard shows application statistics,
  status pipeline, and allows filtering/sorting to stay organized throughout your job hunt.
